# Video storyboard & shot craft rules

Universal cinematic and editing discipline for AI-generated video storyboards, shots, and pacing.

## Kinetic physical action rule (The Verb + Object Rule)

- **Every shot description must specify an active physical verb and its target object**:
  - ❌ Forbidden static prompt: "Character stands by window looking thoughtful for 4 seconds." (Leads to static, lifeless AI video).
  - ✅ Kinetic prompt: "Character turns abruptly toward camera, unrolls blueprint onto wooden desk with force, camera pushes in 15%."
- **Avoid zero-motion dead frames**: Static talking heads without gesture or camera tracking must not exceed 2.0 seconds in viral short-form editing.

## Pacing & visual hierarchy

- **Hook duration**: Shot 1 (Opening Hook) must settle narrative intent within **1.5 to 2.5 seconds**.
- **Shot variation**: Never schedule three consecutive shots of identical framing (e.g. Medium Shot → Medium Shot → Medium Shot). Alternate: Close-up (Hook) → Wide Shot (Context) → Over-the-shoulder (Action).
- **Camera prompt keywords**: Explicitly specify physical motion: `Slow pan left`, `Dynamic push-in`, `Rack focus from foreground object to face`, `Low-angle tracking shot`.
